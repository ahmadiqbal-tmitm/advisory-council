Week 02 · Advisory council

Your own AI advisory council

Socrates, Sun Tzu, Marshall McLuhan and Marcus Aurelius, inside Claude. Add one link, then bring them your next decision.

Want to build your own council? Follow the tutorial.

Step 1: Add the council to Claude (1 minute)

  1. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors.
  2. Click Add custom connector.
  3. Name it AI Advisory Council and paste the address below.
Connector address
https://advisorycouncil.ahmads.build/mcp

Why this matters: a connector is a plug that lets Claude reach the advisors' material. Without it, Claude is only guessing at what they would say.

Step 2: Ask the council (1 minute)

Start a new chat, check the connector is switched on, and paste this with your own decision:

Prompt
Ask the council whether I should ___.

Here is what happens:

  1. Socrates asks you one question to find out what you are really deciding.
  2. After your answer he writes his understanding in five short lines, and suggests which advisors to bring in.
  3. Each advisor answers in their own words, briefly, and usually ends with a question for you.
  4. You see where they agree, where they differ, and which way the council leans.

Reply to any advisor to keep talking with them. In a hurry? Say "skip to the council" to go past Socrates.

Step 3: Or ask one advisor

Name the advisor and you get them alone, with no opening from Socrates:

Prompt
Ask Sun Tzu about this: ___

How it works

  • Four advisors, one field each. Socrates on questioning, Sun Tzu on strategy, Marshall McLuhan on technology and media, Marcus Aurelius on way of life.
  • The council is a library, not a brain. It hands your Claude each advisor's material: their principles, how they speak, and for Socrates and Marcus Aurelius a bank of questions. Your own Claude writes the replies from it.
  • Nothing is stored. There are no accounts. The council keeps no record of what you ask.
  • It is free.
  • It offers perspective, not professional advice. On legal, financial or medical questions, it will point you to a real professional.

See how it is built

The code, the advisors' material and the build log are public on GitHub. Want your own advisors? The tutorial shows how to build a council of your own.

What you'll need

  • A Claude plan that includes custom connectors. Check claude.com/pricing.
  • About two minutes.
  • No code. No account to create.
